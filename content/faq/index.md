## Is this financial advice?

No. SumAI gives general information and estimates based on the numbers you enter. It does not know your full situation and does not recommend what you should do.

## How accurate are the results?

The formulas are standard and tested. The results are only as accurate as the inputs and the assumptions stated with each calculator — for example, a fixed rate for the whole term.

## Where do the rates come from?

Benchmark rates are illustrative market figures used to fill in defaults. Rates on product pages come from providers and may change. Neither is an offer of credit.

## Why is my rate labelled E.A. or APR?

Different countries quote rates in different ways, and the same number can mean a different cost. We label every rate with how it is quoted.

## Does the AI calculate anything?

No. Every figure is computed by deterministic, tested code. The AI only explains figures that have already been computed, and it is instructed never to invent a number.

## Do I need an account?

No. Every calculator works without one. An account lets you save scenarios, compare them, and track goals.

## What happens to my data?

Profile details are optional and used only for eligibility checks and coaching context. They are never required to use a calculator.
