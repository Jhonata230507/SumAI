## What gets financed

The amount you borrow is the vehicle price, plus sales tax and fees, minus your cash down and any trade-in equity.

## Negative equity

If you owe more on your trade-in than it is worth, the difference does not disappear — it is added to the new loan. You start the new loan owing more than the new car is worth.

## Depreciation and being underwater

Cars lose value fastest in the first year. If the loan balance falls more slowly than the car's value, you are "underwater": selling the car would not cover the loan. The calculator estimates when the balance drops below the car's value.

## Assumptions

- About 20% depreciation in the first year, then about 15% a year.
- Fixed rate and on-time payments.
