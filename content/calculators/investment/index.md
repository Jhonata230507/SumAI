## Compounding

Growth earns growth. Over short periods the effect is small. Over decades it dominates: in a long projection, most of the final balance is usually growth rather than contributions.

## Fees

An expense ratio is charged on the whole balance every year, not just on gains. A 1% fee sounds small, but over 25 years it can take a large share of the final balance. The calculator shows the amount lost to fees.

## Inflation

A nominal balance 25 years from now will not buy what the same number buys today. The **today's money** figure adjusts for that.

## A range, not a promise

Returns vary from year to year and are never guaranteed. The calculator shows the same plan at three points lower and higher, so you can see the range.

## Assumptions

- Returns are applied smoothly each month. Real markets are far lumpier.
- Contributions are made at the end of each period.
