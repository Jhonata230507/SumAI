## What the payment is made of

Every instalment loan payment has two parts: **interest** on what you still owe, and **principal** that reduces the balance. Early in the loan the balance is at its highest, so most of each payment is interest. As the balance falls, the split tips toward principal.

That is why the payment stays level while the interest share keeps shrinking.

## Why extra payments work

An extra payment goes entirely to principal. It lowers the balance that every future month charges interest on, so the savings compound for the rest of the loan. Even a small, regular extra amount can cut months off the term.

## Nominal and effective rates

A quoted rate can mean two different things:

- **Nominal annual rate** — common in the United States. A 12% quote is charged as 1% a month, which compounds to about 12.68% a year.
- **Effective annual rate (E.A.)** — common in Colombia. A 12% E.A. already includes compounding, so the monthly rate is about 0.949%.

The calculator converts using the convention of the country you have selected.

## Assumptions

- Fixed rate for the full term.
- Payments on time, every period.
- Origination fees are paid up front and counted in the total cost, not the payment.
