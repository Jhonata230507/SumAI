## The payment is more than the loan

A mortgage payment usually bundles four costs:

1. **Principal and interest** — the loan itself.
2. **Property tax** — often collected monthly by the lender and paid on your behalf.
3. **Home insurance** — the same arrangement.
4. **Mortgage insurance** — required by many lenders when the down payment is small.

Building or HOA fees are paid separately but belong in the monthly budget, so the calculator includes them.

## When mortgage insurance applies

Lenders add mortgage insurance when the down payment falls below a threshold, which varies by country. It usually drops off once the balance falls below 80% of the home value. The calculator estimates when that happens.

## Affordability

Lenders cap how much of your income can go to debt payments. The calculator works backwards from that ceiling to the largest loan your income supports.

## Assumptions

- Fixed rate for the full term.
- Tax and insurance stay at today's amounts.
- Home value is held flat when estimating when mortgage insurance ends.
