## Pay every minimum first

Missing a minimum payment usually costs more in fees and credit damage than any strategy saves. Both approaches below start by paying every minimum.

## Avalanche: highest rate first

Put every extra amount toward the debt with the highest interest rate. This always costs the least interest overall.

## Snowball: smallest balance first

Put every extra amount toward the smallest balance. You clear whole accounts sooner, which many people find easier to stick with.

## Which one?

The avalanche is cheaper; the snowball can be easier to keep going. The difference is often smaller than people expect — the debt payoff calculator shows the exact figure for your debts. The best strategy is the one you will follow.
