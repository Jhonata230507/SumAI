## Start with the monthly cost, not the price

The price is one number. What you will live with is the monthly cost: principal, interest, tax, insurance and fees together. Work that figure out first, then see what price it supports.

## The down payment

A bigger down payment means a smaller loan, a lower payment, and often no mortgage insurance. It also means waiting longer to buy. The savings calculator can show how long reaching a given down payment would take.

## The term

A shorter term means a higher payment and much less interest overall. A longer term lowers the payment but costs more in total. Neither is right for everyone — it depends on what the monthly budget can carry.

## Before you apply

- Know your debt-to-income ratio. Lenders do.
- Check your credit report for errors.
- Keep an emergency fund separate from the down payment.
