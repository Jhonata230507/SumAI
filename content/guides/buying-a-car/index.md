## Think in total cost

A low monthly payment often means a long term, and a long term means more interest. Compare offers on what you would pay in total, including fees.

## Watch the trade-in

If you still owe money on your current car, check what it is worth before you trade it in. Negative equity rolls into the new loan.

## Shorter is usually safer

Cars lose value quickly. A shorter loan keeps the balance falling faster than the car's value, so you are less likely to be underwater if you need to sell.
